05 — Questions

Common questions.

Quick answers before your visit — the things people ask at the counter most.

Do you cut keys while I wait?

Yes, key cutting is a while-you-wait service, no appointment needed.

Do you sell paint in specific colours?

Yes, we can mix paint to a wide range of colours in-store.

Is there parking at the shopping centre?

Yes, Northfield Shopping Centre has customer parking on site, and the shop is easy to reach on foot from the centre entrances.

Do you take card payments?

Yes, card, contactless and cash are all accepted.

Can I check stock before coming in?

Call 07547 741618 and tell us what you’re looking for. A size, product code or a description of the job is all we need.

What if I don’t know what the part is called?

Take a clear photo and note any measurements or markings, then call or bring it in. We’ll work out the rest.

Where can I find you?

Bright Products is in Northfield Shopping Centre, Birmingham. Get directions, or call and we’ll talk you to the door.
